    The chain

    Every link stores its prompt, Claude's response and a hash that includes the link before it. Tap a link to grow it again.

    Words into seeds

    Claude turns your prompt into a sketch made of simple shapes, splits it into up to four living tissues, and picks a color and a behavior for each: membranes, stripes, swarms, mazes.

    Seeds into life

    Every cell runs a small continuous rule that senses its neighbors at three distances. Tissue can only live inside its own part of the sketch, so the drawing holds its shape while its surface keeps moving.

    Links into a chain

    Each prompt is answered with the previous response in hand, and the cells carry over rather than restarting. The scene is the sum of every link so far.

    New links are drawn by Claude using your own Claude usage; you'll be asked to allow it the first time. The first three links are a hand-made example so the page works without it. Cell rules come from the Living Rules search, based on Lenia.
